## Normalization completed

1. **Canonical person names**
   - Known initial/name variants are merged into a single contact identity.
   - Examples: `J. S. Jahr` / `Jahr JS` -> `Jonathan S. Jahr`; `F. Lurie` -> `Fedor Lurie`; `R. A. Gunther` -> `Robert A. Gunther`; `Driessen B` -> `Bernd Driessen`; `Zambelli AB` -> `Anthony B. Zambelli`; `Leisewitz AL` -> `Andrew L. Leisewitz`.

2. **Multi-direction classification**
   - Directions are additive, not exclusive.
   - A person can appear under multiple filters such as `Small Animal + Zoo + Wildlife + One Health` or `Equine + Emergency / Critical Care + Hemorrhagic Shock + Oxygen Delivery`.
   - Direction derivation now also uses publication titles / keywords for publication-only authors.

3. **Species / working groups**
   - Priority logical groups: Wildlife, Zoo, Ocean, Red Book, Bird, Companion, Large Animal.
   - Specific species remain separately searchable.

4. **Contact routes**
   - Public LinkedIn, institutional email, telephone, profile and ORCID can be displayed.
   - Missing routes stay unverified; no inferred emails or identity-matched LinkedIn profiles are added without sufficient evidence.

5. **Publication field**
   - Curated scientific contacts use independently verified `Latest` publication metadata.
   - Conference proceedings and peer-reviewed articles can be distinguished where both exist.
   - Publication-only authors who have not received an independent external check are labelled `Latest in BHOC library`; this must not be interpreted as a globally verified latest publication.
   - Non-publishing executive / investor / philanthropy / operational contacts are marked `Publication: not applicable to this role` rather than `not found`.

6. **Current vs historical identity**
   - Verified current contacts can show `Current verified`.
   - Historical contacts whose 2026 role / identity is not independently resolved show `Historical / current unresolved`.
   - Old publication affiliation is not reused as a current employer without current evidence.

7. **Wildlife / Red Book**
   - The Wildlife page now uses the same multi-direction / contact-route / publication logic as the Veterinary page.
   - People are enriched through the shared `contact-enrichment.json`; organisations and grant programmes derive relevant directions from their public role / focus.
